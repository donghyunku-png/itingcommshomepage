Introducing our GEO practical guide on WikiDocs: being discovered in the AI search era

Explore ITING Communications' Korean-language practical guide to SEO and GEO, global and Chinese AI search, local discovery, question design, measurement and responsible operations.

ITING Communications2026-11-14Updated 2026-11-14

A brand mention, a citation of an official page and a recommendation are different observations. Turning AI search into a practical marketing discipline requires clear questions, inspectable evidence and a repeatable way to decide what to improve.

ITING Communications introduces 『AI 검색 시대의 글로벌 시장에서 발견되고 선택되는 방법』, a Korean-language guide whose title translates as “How to be discovered and chosen in global markets in the AI search era.” Written by Donghyun Ku, it brings together SEO and GEO fundamentals, AI search across Korea, China and global markets, and practical measurement and operations. The linked book is in Korean; this article is an English introduction, not an English edition of the book.

Six parts, from concepts to operations

The public contents checked on November 14, 2026 include six parts and 27 chapters, plus supplementary chapters and appendices.

  • Understanding AI search and GEO: SEO, AEO and GEO; model knowledge, web search, sources and differences between search products.
  • Execution foundations: crawling, indexing, site structure, customer questions, evidence and the limits of FAQs, structured data and llms.txt.
  • Global, country-level and local GEO: separating question language, interface language, location and search mode; maintaining consistent brand facts and market-specific information.
  • GEO in China: examining products and search tools separately, and distinguishing Chinese-language content, social platforms, AI search and AMAP.
  • Measurement and operations: question design; mentions, recommendations, citations and factual accuracy; repeated observation, industry applications and 30-, 60- and 90-day planning.
  • Responsible practice: commercial interests, privacy, copyright, advertising disclosures, uncertainty, unsuccessful tests and change records.

Supplementary chapters address execution by brand-awareness stage, image-only product pages and Naver AI Briefing. Appendices provide a glossary, official documentation references, observation templates and change records. See the current contents for chapter-level updates.

Start with the question your team needs to answer

New to GEO? Begin with the definitions in Part 1. Website teams can use Part 2 and the local GEO chapter. Teams working across markets can start with Parts 3 and 4. If you already monitor AI answers, compare your reporting definitions with Part 5.

A missing company name in one answer to a local recommendation question does not establish why the company was omitted. Checking whether company, service and address information is accurate and readable is one task; recording comparable questions, language, timing and search conditions is another. The guide connects these tasks without treating a single answer as a universal result.

How the book relates to GEO Inside

The book is a learning resource for understanding concepts and building a practical review process. GEO Inside, developed by ITING Communications, connects observation of brand mentions and sources with question design, interpretation and improvement priorities. Reading the book and adopting a commercial solution are separate decisions.

This is a company introduction, and ITING Communications has a commercial interest in GEO Inside. Neither the book nor this article guarantees AI recommendations, citations, rankings or revenue. Changes in observed answers do not by themselves establish that a website edit caused them. Readers should review sources and updates before applying guidance, because platform capabilities and policies can change.
