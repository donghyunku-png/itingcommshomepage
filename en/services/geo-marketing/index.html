GEO marketing · GEO Inside

ITING Communications provides generative engine optimization (GEO) diagnosis and consulting through its own GEO Inside platform. We examine what AI answers say and which sources they use when customers discover, compare, and choose brands, then identify improvements to official information and content.

For businesses that want to understand how their brand is described in AI search, identify missing or inaccurate information, and compare competing brands with evidence.

What we work on

Frequently asked questions

How do SEO and GEO differ?
SEO helps search engines discover and understand pages. GEO builds on that foundation by clarifying official information and sources that answer customer questions and observing changes in generative AI answers. We review them together.
Do you guarantee recommendations in ChatGPT, Gemini, or Claude?
No. External services decide search, citations, and answers, which vary by question, time, region, account, and environment. Site improvements support discovery and understanding but do not guarantee mentions, citations, rankings, inquiries, or revenue.
Which engines does GEO Inside measure?
The current repeated-measurement set is ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity. Naver's AI tab is observed separately. Claude and Chinese platforms are not presented as native measurement integrations.
Does AI search require a special file or schema?
Google says its AI features do not require special files or dedicated schema. We first check crawl access, search indexing, helpful text, internal links, and structured data that matches visible content.
