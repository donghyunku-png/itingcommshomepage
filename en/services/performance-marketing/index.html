Performance marketing · Digital advertising

ITING Communications connects digital advertising strategy, content planning, media operations, and data analysis around customer journeys and business goals. Performance marketing reviews agreed conversions such as inquiries or purchases alongside impressions and clicks to identify the next improvements.

For businesses, including smaller businesses, that want to run search, social, or video campaigns and clarify which messages and landing pages to improve and how to interpret performance.

What we work on

  • Campaign strategy and content direction aligned with business goals and customer journeys
  • Search, social, video, and programmatic media operations
  • Conversion definitions, measurement plans, dashboards, and reporting
  • Consistency between advertising messages, landing pages, and inquiry journeys

Scope and conditions

Media, ad spend, duration, production, and measurement requirements determine scope and pricing. These are components to agree during consultation, not a fixed inclusion list for every project. Specific ROAS, CPA, inquiry volume, or revenue is not guaranteed.

How a project proceeds

  1. 01

    Define goals and conversions

    Review customers, products or services, campaign goals, and conversion criteria.

  2. 02

    Plan media and content

    Set channel roles, creative direction, and landing-page connections.

  3. 03

    Operate and review data

    Operate within the agreed scope and check collection conditions and metrics.

  4. 04

    Interpret and improve

    Explain observed differences and limitations and propose priorities for the next steps.

Deliverables to agree during consultation

  • An operating plan covering campaign goals, media, and measurement criteria
  • Agreed media operations and performance reports
  • Prioritized creative, landing-page, and conversion-flow improvements

Frequently asked questions

Which advertising channels do you cover?
Our service areas include search, social, video, and programmatic media. The actual channels and scope depend on goals, budget, accounts, and measurement conditions.
How is performance evaluated?
We agree on conversions and metrics that fit the campaign goal. We distinguish impressions and clicks from conversion data and consider periods, platform attribution rules, and possible data gaps.
Can this be combined with GEO marketing?
Digital advertising and GEO Inside diagnosis can be considered together within an agreed scope. Paid-media performance and AI-answer observations are different metrics and are evaluated separately.
How are budget and timing decided?
We review goals, products or services, target customers, preferred timing, channels, and available materials during consultation. We do not publish a fixed price or guaranteed performance figure.
