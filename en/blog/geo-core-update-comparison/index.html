After a Google Core Update, Separate Rankings from AI Citations

A practical way to mark the rollout period and compare Google Search performance, AI citations, and site traffic as separate observations.

ITING Communications2026-11-25Updated 2026-11-25

You edit a service page. The next day its Google ranking rises, and an AI answer mentions your company. Both are useful observations. Neither tells you that the edit caused the change. When search systems are changing, start by separating when you measured from what you measured.

Mark the rollout as a separate period

Google’s November 2026 core update began on November 10 and finished on November 24 at 6:03 a.m. Pacific time, or 11:03 p.m. in Korea. Google said the ranking update applied globally and to all languages. The announcement does not explain movements on individual sites or changes in any particular AI answer. [1]

Google recommends waiting at least a full week after completion, then comparing that week with one before the rollout. Record the dashboard timestamps and check the time zone used by your reports. Keep the rollout data, but do not mix it into either comparison window. [2]

Measure three different things

  1. Search performance: In Search Console, use consistent search type, country, page, and query filters when comparing impressions, clicks, and average position. An aggregate can conceal which pages actually moved.
  2. AI answers: Repeat the same question with the same engine, language, and location settings. Record whether the brand was mentioned, which URL was cited, and whether that page supports the specific claim.
  3. Site traffic: Google includes traffic from its AI search features in the Web search type in Search Console. The aggregate does not isolate clicks from those features or tell you what is happening in other AI services. [3]

If impressions rise and citations do not change across the sampled answers, report the rise in impressions on its own. If an answer links to a new but irrelevant service page, check whether the page supports the claim before treating the link as a win. These are examples, not measured results.

What to record today

Site change and date / observation window / search type or AI engine / query or prompt / URL / result / remaining uncertainty. Record the rollout and your own edits now, then repeat the comparison once the window is complete. Fix a broken page or an incorrect product fact when you find it, but avoid rewriting working pages solely because a few days of rankings moved.
