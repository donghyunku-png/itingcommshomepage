# AI Search Clicks: Separate Estimates from Measured Results

## What should we check after appearing in an AI answer?

Your brand appears in an AI answer. What belongs in the next performance report? A recorded mention, a website visit, and a customer inquiry represent different stages. Separating them helps explain what has been observed and what remains unknown.

A recently published experiment offers a useful starting point. The important question is how the number was produced, rather than whether it should become your next target.

## The estimated 21% share comes from one site

Cyrus Shepard excluded Zyppy from Google’s AI search features for 28 days beginning September 1, 2026, then compared that period with 28 days after opting back in. Using comparisons that also matched queries and ranking positions, he estimated that AI features produced about 21% of the site’s Google search clicks. This was an inferred contribution, not a separately recorded click count, from one experiment on one site. [Read the original methods and findings](https://signal.zyppy.com/p/we-opted-out-of-google-ai-search)

This is not an industry average or evidence of revenue impact. Comparing different time periods also leaves other possible influences to consider.

## Start with the data you can actually see

Google’s official help page, checked on November 11, 2026, describes an AI performance report providing impressions for AI Overviews and AI Mode, with dimensions including pages, countries, and devices. It does not describe a separate AI-click metric. Google also notes that insufficient impressions can explain why a site does not see the report. [Read the report documentation](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/16984139)

We suggest keeping these observations separate:

- Answer observation: Which service and question showed the brand or a link?
- Search performance: What impressions and total search clicks appear in the available reports?
- Website outcomes: Did a visitor complete a defined action, such as an inquiry or purchase?
- Estimates: Which unobserved quantities were calculated using assumptions or comparisons?

Separate fields make it harder to mistake more appearances for more inquiries. Where a measure cannot be verified, label it unknown or estimated rather than entering zero.

## Record what else changed

For a weekly comparison, start with consistent dates, pages, country, and device conditions. Keep branded searches separate from general recommendation questions. Record any change to the questions or pages being assessed.

Also note advertising, price changes, content updates, and site errors during the same period. If inquiries rise while advertising expands, attributing the entire increase to AI visibility would need more evidence. This is a hypothetical interpretation example, not a client result.

A useful reporting row can contain the observed change, other changed conditions, and unresolved causes. Its purpose is to decide what to investigate next, rather than hide uncertainty.

## Consider traffic risk before copying the experiment

Google says excluding a site from its generative search features prevents its links and content from appearing there, removing impressions and traffic from those features. This setting is separate from AI-training controls. [Read Google’s control guidance](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/16908024)

We do not recommend casually excluding a live site to estimate clicks. Begin with a baseline from accessible reports and inquiry records. If an experiment is necessary, review the potential loss, observation period, and reversal criteria with the responsible team. An interesting external experiment still needs a separate decision before it becomes an operational change.

## Label the type of number in your next report

Choose one main service page this week and record answer observations, search performance, and actual inquiries separately. Attach a date range and source to each dataset, and distinguish measured values from estimates. Rechecking under consistent conditions provides a clearer basis for deciding which information or page to improve first.

A useful GEO report makes the limits of its evidence visible. Build a record of what changed for your brand instead of promising another site’s percentage.
