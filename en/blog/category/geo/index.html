A practical starting point for GEO

Generative engine optimization starts with clear brand information and observing how AI answers use it. A brand mention, a cited source, a website visit and a purchase are different outcomes.

  1. Define customer questions, language, engines and the observation period.
  2. Check official product facts, crawl access and visible sources.
  3. Repeat observations under comparable conditions and distinguish uncertainty from traffic and sales.

These articles support diagnosis and improvement; they do not guarantee AI recommendations, search rankings or revenue.
