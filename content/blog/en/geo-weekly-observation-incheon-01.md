# Looking for a GEO Provider in Incheon: Week 1 of Our GEO Observations

We looked at how our company, ITING Communications, appeared in answers for someone looking for a GEO marketing provider in Incheon. This is a self-observation from November 24, 2026. It is not a customer campaign result or a ranking of providers.

## The answers differed with the scope of the question

When we asked for an Incheon provider specializing in **GEO marketing solutions**, Gemini mentioned ITING Communications as an option. ITING did not appear in the list of local providers we reviewed in the ChatGPT answer to that question. When we asked for an Incheon provider that offers **both performance marketing and GEO**, both services mentioned ITING.

The location stayed the same, but the two prompts asked for different services. These are the answers we saw, not evidence of consistent visibility or comparative rankings. If an answer shows a source link, we still need to open the original page and check which claim it supports. [3][4]

## What we have clarified on our site

We clarified the distinction between our Songdo headquarters in Incheon and our Seoul office, and made it easier to see what the GEO service examines and delivers. We also linked the company profile, service description and related reading. [1][2] Those changes preceded this observation, but we have not established whether any one change influenced the answers.

In the next installment, we will keep the two questions separate. Alongside whether our name appears, we will check whether the location and services are described accurately and which pages, if any, are offered as evidence.
