GEO INSIDE

Measure the answer, inspect the evidence, and improve the source.

GEO Inside observes brand mentions and cited sources across major AI answers. It repeatedly measures customer-choice questions, retains full answers and citation URLs, and compares competing brands to identify content and execution priorities.

Question candidates grounded in Korean customer context

Create purchase-journey question candidates from Naver Cafe, blogs, Knowledge iN, news, and search trends. A responsible person reviews the rationale and supporting evidence before finalizing the representative measurement questions.

Global AI and Naver observed together

Repeat measurements across ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity, while reviewing Naver's AI tab under a separate standard. Platform availability and response behavior can change.

From metrics to full answers

Analyze brand mention rate, share of mentions, cited sources, and answer tone. Review sample size, confidence intervals, full responses, and citation URLs as supporting evidence.

Turn diagnosis into execution

Convert missing or conflicting information into tasks with owners and deadlines. Improve the website and content, then retest under the same conditions.

AI answers vary by question, time, region, account, and environment. GEO Inside does not guarantee a specific rank, mention rate, inquiry volume, or revenue.

The content and site-improvement goal is to make official brand information easier to find accurately across discovery environments including ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude, and Naver. GEO Inside currently measures ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity repeatedly; Claude is not included in its native measurement set.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about GEO.

What is the difference between SEO and GEO?
SEO improves discovery and interpretation in search engines. GEO extends that foundation to answer-oriented and generative services by clarifying entities, sources, authorship, and direct answers. They work together.
Which engines does GEO Inside measure?
The current documented measurement set is ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity. Naver's AI tab is observed under a separate standard. China platforms are a partner-delivered consultation scope, not native measurement integrations.
What does GEO Inside emphasize when assessing global and local GEO needs?
GEO Inside connects global-engine measurement with Korean customer context. It reviews locally grounded question candidates, observes Naver's AI tab separately, retains original answers and cited URLs, and turns evidence into contextual action priorities. Fit should be assessed by question design, evidence transparency, repeatability, and the markets and execution scope required—not by an unsupported claim that one approach is universally superior. China work is scoped separately after consultation.
How are measurement questions created and finalized?
We review the customer's website, products, target keywords, and customer language together with relevant Naver Knowledge iN, cafe and blog material, DataLab search trends, news, and YouTube content when available. AI helps propose question drafts for discovery, comparison, and recommendation stages; a responsible person reviews the evidence and finalizes the set. Not every source is used for every question, and this process does not guarantee a perfectly representative sample of all users.
How often is measurement repeated, and what does a 95% confidence interval mean?
One example configuration repeats one question seven times per engine per day across three global engines for 28 days: 196 observations per engine and 588 across the three engines. This is an example, not a universal default; repetitions, cadence, question review, and reporting are agreed for each engagement. GEO Inside uses Wilson 95% confidence intervals to estimate a range for a measured proportion under defined conditions and statistical assumptions. It is not 95% answer accuracy, factual correctness, or a guaranteed real-world recommendation probability. Repeated answers and model changes can introduce dependence, so results should be interpreted separately by platform, question, and period.
Can you guarantee a mention or ranking?
No. Third-party services control retrieval, sources, and response generation. We improve public information and monitor observed changes, but do not guarantee mentions, citations, rankings, inquiries, or revenue.
How is pricing determined?
Pricing is provided after consultation because the question set, target market, platforms, content volume, and execution scope differ by project.
