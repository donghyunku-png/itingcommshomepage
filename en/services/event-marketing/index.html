ITING SERVICES

Event planning · Exhibitions · Operations

ITING Communications plans and operates exhibitions, corporate promotions, pop-up stores, and sports and esports events. We shape spaces and programs around the event's purpose and support on-site operations, with content, social, and advertising scope considered when needed.

For businesses preparing direct brand experiences and seeking to align event goals, programs, on-site operations, and pre- and post-event communication.

What we work on

  • Planning and operations for exhibitions, corporate events, and promotions
  • Pop-up store spaces, programs, and on-site operational support
  • Sports and esports event planning and operations
  • Planning to connect event experiences with website, video, short-form, and social content

Scope and conditions

Venue, dates, scale, production, and operational conditions determine scope and pricing. Facility, safety, permits, filming, and image-rights requirements and responsibilities are reviewed before contracting. Public reference names do not establish the scope or results of any specific event.

How a project proceeds

  1. 01

    Review event requirements

    Confirm goals, participants, preferred dates and venue, budget, and required operations.

  2. 02

    Plan spaces and programs

    Organize spaces and programs around the message and visitor experience.

  3. 03

    Agree on responsibilities

    Define on-site operations, production, content, external collaboration, and responsibilities for the project.

  4. 04

    Connect follow-up content

    Review records and materials cleared for publication and connect them with follow-up content and improvements.

Deliverables to agree during consultation

  • A proposal covering event goals, spaces, and programs
  • An agreed operational scope and responsibility plan
  • Follow-up content planning using event records cleared for publication

Frequently asked questions

What types of events do you cover?
Our service areas include exhibitions, corporate events, promotions, pop-up stores, sports, and esports. Dates, region, scale, and delivery scope are confirmed after consultation.
Can event work include online advertising and content?
Event planning and operations can be considered alongside digital advertising, video, short-form, and social content. Filming, production, ad spend, and channel operations are agreed separately.
What information helps with a proposal?
Share the event's goal, participants and expected scale, preferred dates and venue, budget range, required work, and available materials to help define the scope.
Can I see detailed reference projects?
Company names on the website identify approved references only. They do not imply a specific scope, result, testimonial, or endorsement. Available case details can be discussed during consultation.
