Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-11-04 | Author: ITING Communications Privacy Officer

In summary, this policy explains which personal data ITING Communications Inc. (the “Company”) processes, for what purpose, and for how long, in line with Article 30 of the Korean Personal Information Protection Act.

1. What personal data do we collect?

  • Website inquiry fields: name, company (optional), email, topic, message, and privacy-consent status
  • Technical information Formspree may automatically process for service operation, security, and spam prevention: IP address, browser type, domain, access time, and referring-page address
  • The website currently does not install a separate visitor-analytics script or advertising cookies.

2. Why do we collect personal data?

We use the information to deliver and reply to inquiries, provide consultation or quotes, prevent abuse and spam, secure the service, and comply with applicable law. Website-form submissions are transmitted through Formspree to sales@itingcomms.com. Do not enter sensitive personal data such as health, financial, or government-identification information.

3. How long do we keep the data?

  • Email inquiry data: 1 year after the reply and consultation purpose is fulfilled
  • Formspree Free submission archive: submissions are available in the dashboard for 30 days. This is the dashboard archive-availability period, not a guarantee that every backup or security log is deleted after 30 days. Other processing follows Formspree's policies.
  • Contracted clients: 5 years per the e-Commerce Consumer Protection Act

4. Third-party sharing and processors

We use Formspree, Inc. to transmit website inquiries, provide dashboard archiving, and process spam and security signals. Formspree may process information in the United States and other countries where it operates. The Free plan has a 50-submission monthly limit; over-limit submissions may be held with limited previews while notification emails and plugins are paused. For details, see the Formspree Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

5. What are your rights as a data subject?

In conclusion, you may request access, correction, deletion, or processing restriction at any time by emailing sales@itingcomms.com, and we will respond without undue delay.
