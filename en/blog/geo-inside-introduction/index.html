Introducing GEO Inside AI Search Diagnostics and Platform

GEO Inside is a GEO solution developed by ITING Communications. It examines brand mentions and sources in AI answers and identifies areas to improve website information and content.

ITING Communications (developer of GEO Inside)2026-11-09Updated 2026-11-09

GEO Inside is a GEO solution developed by ITING Communications. It examines how brands are mentioned and described in answers from major generative AI services such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity, and identifies areas to improve website information and content. It combines a platform for observing AI answers with services that help design questions, interpret findings and define next steps.

Customers consult AI answers alongside search results when choosing products or comparing services. For a company, checking whether its name appears once is not enough. It also needs to understand which questions lead to mentions, whether product information is accurate and which sources the AI presents. GEO Inside connects these questions to a brand's marketing and content work.

GEO addresses brand information in AI answers

GEO stands for Generative Engine Optimization. It means organizing information and content so that brands and products can be understood accurately and used as references in environments where generative AI finds information and answers questions.

It is related to SEO, which helps search engines find pages, and AEO, which provides clear answers to customer questions. However, search rankings, mentions in AI answers and source citations are different outcomes. GEO Inside examines them separately rather than using a single number to judge a brand's overall position.

Three questions for brands to check first

  • Does AI mention our brand? Check whether the brand appears and how it compares with competitors across customer discovery, comparison and recommendation questions.
  • Does AI describe our company and products accurately? Compare official information with AI answers to review outdated or incorrect descriptions.
  • Which materials support the answers? Examine the sources AI presents alongside the official website to identify information gaps, inconsistencies and accessibility issues.

GEO Inside as a solution

GEO Inside connects observations of AI answers with brand diagnostics. It distinguishes questions where a brand is visible from those where it is not adequately explained, and compares the evidence presented in answers with information on the website. This helps identify areas to review first, including official information, comparison content, selection criteria and evidence of expertise.

For example, if key product specifications appear only in images, that information may also need to be available as readable text. If pages do not address common comparison questions, a company can consider content with verifiable product information and selection criteria. These are examples of diagnostic recommendations, not claims about results achieved for a particular brand.

GEO Inside as a service

The work begins with deciding which questions to measure. The brand's website, main products and strengths, target customers, competitors and language used by actual customers inform the design of observation questions. Questions containing a brand name and general recommendation questions serve different purposes and are interpreted separately.

A reviewer examines the observations and organizes actionable recommendations and priorities. Marketing, PR and website teams can use those findings to discuss the scope of work. Content production, website development or changes, and PR execution are discussed separately from the diagnostic work.

GEO Inside as a platform

The GEO Inside platform provides a basis for examining AI answers, brand mentions, competitive context and sources for each question. It supports comparing summary figures with the original answers and reviewing differences across observation conditions and dates.

The same AI can give different answers to the same question. Rather than treating one screenshot as the complete result, observations are repeated with defined conditions such as the period, questions and engines. Results from ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity are interpreted separately from observations of Naver's AI tab. Detailed measurement coverage and reporting methods are confirmed during onboarding.

After diagnostics, review both changes and the work performed

After improving brand information, record what changed and when, then observe the same questions again. This process checks whether newly published or updated pages appear as sources and whether descriptions of the brand have changed.

A change in observed metrics cannot automatically be attributed to content updates. AI model changes, observation dates and competitors' activity may also affect results. GEO Inside considers these conditions to help inform the next action.

Who can use GEO Inside

GEO Inside can help companies understand how AI introduces their brands, teams check inconsistencies between their official websites and external information, and managers set priorities before creating content. Agencies carrying out GEO work for client brands can also discuss roles and operating scope.

Common questions before getting started

Does it guarantee first place in AI search or recommendations?

No. Service providers cannot control generative AI answers or search results. GEO Inside aims to identify areas for improvement and track changes based on observed evidence.

Are content production and website changes included?

Diagnostics and recommended next steps are defined first. Responsibility, scope and cost for production, development, changes and execution are discussed separately.

How do we get started?

Prepare your website address, main products or services, target customers and the questions you want to investigate. An initial consultation defines the observation scope, operating approach and required materials.
